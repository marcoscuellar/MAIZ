10
Human control
built into the system

Confidence
is cheap.

Most AI tools are designed to produce an answer. MAIZ is designed to produce work your team can inspect, challenge, and decide whether to act on.

A built-in adversary

Engine 03

A separate engine tries to disprove the finding before it moves forward. It sees the sources, not the story. Signals that don't survive don't move.

Missing beats fabricated

No engine may invent a fact, date, title, or source. When information is unavailable, the system writes around it or sends the work back upstream. It never smooths over the gap.

Approval is not send

Nothing is emailed, messaged, or dispatched without a separate, explicit instruction from your team. MAIZ prepares the work. Your person decides what leaves the building.
